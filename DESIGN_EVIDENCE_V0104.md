| Use subtle elevation and press feedback rather than neon/AI decoration | ShowRunner brand guardrails and CDO direction | High | Yes |
| Preserve visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion behavior | WCAG 2.2 / accessibility foundation | Very high | No |
